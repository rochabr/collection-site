Rosina Becker do Valle was a painter from Rio de Janeiro who began devoting herself to painting in the 1950s after attending Ivan Serpa’s course at the Museum of Modern Art in Rio de Janeiro. She participated in national salons, two editions of the São Paulo Biennial and exhibitions in Brazil and abroad. Her work is represented in the MASP collection.
