Born in Finland, Eila Helena Ampula moved to Brazil at the age of 13. Associated with Penedo, she developed paintings and tapestries depicting Brazilian subjects. She began experimenting with weaving in the 1960s, working with her husband, Martti Ampula. The Art Museum of the Federal University of Ceará presented an exhibition of her tapestries in 1970.
