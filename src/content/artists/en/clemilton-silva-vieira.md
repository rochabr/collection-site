Clemilton Silva Vieira is an artisan from the Mata da Onça settlement, in the Ilha do Ferro district (Pão de Açúcar, Alagoas), on the banks of the São Francisco River. A natural-born artist, he works mainly with reclaimed wood, making sculptures and paintings that depict everyday scenes, mythological figures, people, animals and landscapes, without adhering to fixed patterns.

In recent years he has also taken up painting, using doors and windows salvaged from demolitions as his canvases. Recognized as one of the notable names of Alagoas folk art, he finds his greatest laboratory and source of inspiration in his own community.
