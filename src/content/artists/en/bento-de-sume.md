Born in Sumé, Paraíba, Bento's first contact with wood was in the fields, and later making charcoal. While working in São Paulo, he suffered an accident that left his physical mobility limited. It was there that he began looking again at the wood around him; he also returned to his hometown and let faith and the inspiration of nature guide his artistic practice.

Motivated mainly by sacred art and animal forms, Bento de Sumé's sculptures have strong characteristics, with his style being easily recognizable.
