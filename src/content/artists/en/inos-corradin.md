Inos Corradin (1929–2025) was a painter, draughtsman, printmaker, sculptor and set designer, born in Vogogna, Italy. He moved as a child to Castelbaldo, near Padua, where he studied painting. In 1950 he emigrated to Brazil, settling in Jundiaí and São Paulo, where he joined the Cooperativa artistic circle led by the Argentine painter Oswaldo Gil Navarro.

He designed sets for the Ballet of São Paulo's Fourth Centenary (1954) and, in 1979, for the Teatro di Rovigo in Italy. He held numerous solo exhibitions and took part in group shows and official Salons in Brazil and abroad, winning prizes in Paris (1975) and Ferrara (1976). Based in São Paulo throughout his career, he belongs to Brazilian modernist figuration.
